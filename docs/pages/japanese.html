Japanese for Learn or Die: Language Teacher. A complete mod: the Learn or Die teacher and quizzes plus everything for Japanese.

Your teacher watches what you do in Minecraft and says it out loud in Japanese: 私は土を掘っています watashi wa tsuchi o hotte imasu, "I am digging the dirt". The sentence shows up on screen in Japanese script with its romaji reading and the English translation. Then she quizzes you: answer right and you get buffs, answer wrong and you get debuffs.

What's inside

  • 2,911 spoken phrases about what you do: walking, mining, chopping trees, placing blocks, eating, fighting, sleeping, the weather and more. Counted items the natural way: the sentence first, the number with its counter word after it (3本, 15枚).
  • 515 words in polite, everyday Japanese: kanji and kana as they are really written, colour adjectives (赤い羊毛, "red wool"), particles は, を and に coloured by their role in the sentence, and the right counter word for every item.
  • Romaji under every sentence (hold Right Shift), so you can read along from day one, and a Japanese font that draws the characters properly.
  • A calm, young, studio-quality voice from Japan at two speeds, normal and slow (press K). Works offline.
  • Voice answers in Japanese. Say the phrase into your microphone; the speech recognition runs on your own computer (set it up in the mod's settings).

How to install

  1. Download the file for your Minecraft version, 1.21.1 or 26.3. The same file works on Fabric and NeoForge.
  2. Put it into your mods folder and start the game. With more than one language, choose it in the settings: press I → Language.

With the CurseForge App, just install this mod into a Fabric or NeoForge profile of Minecraft 1.21.1 or 26.3: the app picks the right file.

The file is about 90 MB because all the teacher's speech is inside it: no internet is needed to play.
