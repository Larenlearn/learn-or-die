English for Learn or Die: Language Teacher. A complete mod: the Learn or Die teacher and quizzes plus everything for English.

Your teacher watches what you do in Minecraft and says it out loud in English: I am digging the dirt. The sentence shows up on screen with a translation in your own language, taken from the language your Minecraft is set to: Spanish, Portuguese, German, Chinese or Japanese (other languages see the English sentence only). Then he quizzes you: answer right and you get buffs, answer wrong and you get debuffs.

What's inside

  • 2,719 spoken phrases about what you do: walking, mining, chopping trees, placing blocks, eating, fighting, sleeping, the weather and more. Counted items the natural way: the sentence first, the number after it.
  • 515 words, the real names of the blocks, creatures and items, with the colour words and verbs coloured by their role in the sentence.
  • A clear, confident American voice at two speeds, normal and slow (press K). Works offline.
  • Voice answers in English. Say the phrase into your microphone; the speech recognition runs on your own computer (set it up in the mod's settings).
  • Translations in five languages chosen by your game's language setting (Options → Language): Spanish, Portuguese, German, Chinese and Japanese.

How to install

  1. Download the file for your Minecraft version, 1.21.1 or 26.3. The same file works on Fabric and NeoForge.
  2. Put it into your mods folder and start the game. With more than one language, choose it in the settings: press I → Language.

With the CurseForge App, just install this mod into a Fabric or NeoForge profile of Minecraft 1.21.1 or 26.3: the app picks the right file.

The file is about 70 MB because all the teacher's speech is inside it: no internet is needed to play.
