Spanish for Learn or Die: Language Teacher. A complete mod: the Learn or Die teacher and quizzes plus everything for Spanish.

Your teacher watches what you do in Minecraft and says it out loud in Spanish: Estoy cavando la tierra, "I am digging the dirt". The sentence shows up on screen with its English translation. Then she quizzes you: answer right and you get buffs, answer wrong and you get debuffs.

What's inside

  • 2,783 spoken phrases about what you do: walking, mining, chopping trees, placing blocks, eating, fighting, sleeping, the weather and more. Counted items the natural way: the sentence first, the number after it.
  • 515 words with real grammar: articles, genders, plurals and colours that agree with the noun. Tiré la lana roja, "I dropped the red wool".
  • A young, studio-quality voice from Spain at two speeds, normal and slow (press K). Works offline.
  • Voice answers in Spanish. Say the phrase into your microphone; the speech recognition runs on your own computer (set it up in the mod's settings).

How to install

  1. Download the file for your Minecraft version, 1.21.1 or 26.3. The same file works on Fabric and NeoForge.
  2. Put it into your mods folder and start the game. With more than one language, choose it in the settings: press I → Language.

With the CurseForge App, just install this mod into a Fabric or NeoForge profile of Minecraft 1.21.1 or 26.3: the app picks the right file.

The file is about 75 MB because all the teacher's speech is inside it: no internet is needed to play.
